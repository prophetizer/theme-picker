Day/night schedule on · now day (catppuccin-latte) · next: catppuccin-mocha at 19:30

A theme picked by hand stays until the next switch time; then the schedule takes over again. Scheduled switches are not announced on ntfy. Sunrise and sunset are worked out here, from the coordinates (kept to 2 decimals, about 1 km); nothing is looked up online. Use a negative offset for earlier.
